# Sources of the vocabulary content in `schemas/`

`schemas/vocabularies.json` and the profile schemas contain pick-lists, term identifiers (IRIs) and names that come from other organisations' vocabularies. This table says what was taken from where, and what is known about the licence. Written 30 October 2026 from the identifiers actually present in the files (counted by search), not from memory of what might be included.

Licence findings come from `_work/clio/2026-10-30-vocab-licences.md` (a second pass of about eight fetches, not legal advice). "Not checked" means nobody has looked; "not confirmed" means a page was read and no licence statement was found. Neither means there is no licence. Confirm the rest before the repository is made public, and keep the credit lines below.

| Source | Publisher | What is in `schemas/` | Licence, as found |
|---|---|---|---|

| Dublin Core Terms (`purl.org/dc/terms`) | DCMI | Term IRIs for rights, licence, references and the like | CC BY 4.0 according to DCMI. Not re-checked for this table. |
| CGI Simple Lithology (`resource.geosciml.org/classifier/cgi/lithology`) | CGI Geoscience Terminology Working Group (IUGS) | About 33 concept IRIs and their labels as pick-list values | CC BY 4.0, stated in the CGI vocabularies repository README ("CGI's vocabularies in this repository are licensed using the CC BY 4.0 licence", <https://github.com/CGI-IUGS/cgi-vocabs>, checked 30 Oct 2026; the ARDC registry lists the older v0.1 as CC-BY, 3.0 AU). | International Chronostratigraphic Chart (`resource.geosciml.org/classifier/ics/ischart`) | International Commission on Stratigraphy (ICS), published through CGI | **IRIs only.** About 27 concept IRIs, as a list in `vocabularies.json` and in the `x-clio-vocabularyIris` annotation of the two age fields. Episodes 36, 199-204." **Known remainder:** the two geology age fields (`ageEarliest`, `ageLatest`) in the geological-sample profiles are pick-lists whose enum values are the common English names of the periods (Jurassic, Cretaceous...). They stay in the profile schemas because changing them would change an immutable, published profile version; they are this project's local pick-list codes, not ICS's labels, but they are the same words. A later profile version should use neutral codes or IRIs. Decision for Rob / Clio. |
